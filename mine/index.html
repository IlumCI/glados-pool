Mine in your browser

Your spare PC can mine $GLaDOS right here in this tab, and real-world assets too. Give it your wallet, pick what you want to be paid in, and press start. It mines on your CPU, and on your graphics card where the browser allows. Nothing starts until you press start, and closing the tab stops it. What you earn lands straight in your wallet on Robinhood Chain, with nothing to claim.

How you get paid

  • Everyone gets the same. Each payout is split equally between every eligible wallet, and each share is paid in what that wallet chose.
  • Who is eligible: any wallet that did at least a quarter of the typical miner's work that round. Miss a round and your work carries to the next. Holding 1,000,000 $GLaDOS is what unlocks being paid in a stock or a basket; under that you are paid in $GLaDOS.
  • How often: about every 30 minutes, as soon as the pot is large enough that fees stay under a tenth of it. More miners means more frequent payouts.
  • No fixed price per share. Your work is sold at live market rates, so what a share is worth changes by the minute. The figure above is the real measure, not a quoted rate.
  • Straight to your wallet on Robinhood Chain. There is nothing to claim.

All it ever needs is your address. It never asks for a key or a signature. Every payout, and its transaction, is published at payouts.json.
